# Security policy

DVTEE is experimental and unaudited. It has not run on SEV-SNP hardware and must not hold
mainnet keys. It is out of scope for the
[Obol bug bounty](https://docs.obol.org/advanced-and-troubleshooting/security/bug-bounty).

Reports are still welcome. Please disclose responsibly through Obol's
[security contact process](https://docs.obol.org/advanced-and-troubleshooting/security/contact)
rather than in a public issue. The gaps we already know about are listed in
[ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md#known-gaps).

The threat model, including what the design trusts, is in [docs/threat-model.md](docs/threat-model.md).
